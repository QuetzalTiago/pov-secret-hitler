# Sound credits

All files here were trimmed, peak-normalised to -1 dBFS and re-encoded as 128 kbps MP3 with ffmpeg.

| File | Source | Author | License | Changes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `gunshot.mp3` | [The Free Firearm Sound Library](https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library), Smith & Wesson 642 revolver, `V_22P.wav` | Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras, Matthew Nanney | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) | one shot cut out (0.62–2.70 s), fades |
| `death-1.mp3`, `death-2.mp3`, `death-3.mp3` | [grunts of male death and pain](https://opengameart.org/content/grunts-male-death-and-pain) | thebardofblasphemy | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) | three takes cut out, fades |
| `ja.mp3` | [File:De-ja.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:De-ja.ogg), Wikimedia Commons | MiER | [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/) (also GFDL) | silence trimmed, light room echo added |
| `nein.mp3` | [File:De-nein.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:De-nein.ogg), Wikimedia Commons | Lars Engelmann (LarsvonSpeck) | [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/) | silence trimmed, light room echo added |

Every other sound in the game is synthesized in code (`client/audio.ts`).
