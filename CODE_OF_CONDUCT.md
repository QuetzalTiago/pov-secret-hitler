# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, assume good intent, keep feedback about the work rather than the person,
and leave harassment, insults and personal attacks out of every project space (issues, pull requests,
discussions and in-game chat on the hosted site).

Secret Hitler is a game about a dark period of history. Playing the game's roles is part of the fun; real
hate speech, glorification of fascism or harassment are not, and will be removed.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening a confidential
[security advisory](https://github.com/QuetzalTiago/pov-secret-hitler/security/advisories/new) (it is private to
the maintainers) or by contacting the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and temporarily or permanently ban
contributors, for behaviour they deem harmful.
